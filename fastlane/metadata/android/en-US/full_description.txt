<p>BetterLauncher is a free, open-source Android launcher with a text-based home screen. Put your favorite apps and shortcuts within reach, without ads or paid upgrades.</p>
<p><b>Make it yours.</b> Arrange up to eight apps and eight shortcuts. Add folders and an Android widget, adjust fonts and alignment, and make labels easier to read over your wallpaper.</p>
<p><b>Find and open apps.</b> Search the app drawer with precise or flexible matching. You can also search Android settings and, with permission, your contacts.</p>
<p><b>Take breaks from distracting apps.</b> Set optional cooldowns or require device authentication when opening selected apps from BetterLauncher. Save or restore your settings with a JSON backup.</p>
<p><b>Optional accessibility service.</b> Double-tap screen lock, app cooldowns and optional Xiaomi gestures use Android Accessibility. The service uses window changes and the foreground app to enforce cooldowns and can read on-screen view information to recognize the lock gesture. This processing stays on your device. You can turn the service off and keep using the rest of the launcher.</p>
<p>BetterLauncher has no Android Internet permission or analytics. If you share a backup, Android hands it to the app or service you choose. Licensed under GPL-3.0.</p>
